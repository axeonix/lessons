## Introduction

Have you ever heard of four angels who are right now — at this very moment — imprisoned? Not just forgotten or ignored — but chained, bound, waiting for a specific hour, on a specific day, in a specific month, in a specific year...to be released. This might sound like something out of an apocalyptic movie or ancient legend — but this is not fiction. This is Scripture. This is prophecy. This is the Word of God.

Let's take a look at one of the most terrifying and fascinating mysteries in The Book of Revelation — four fallen angels, bound at the great river Euphrates, waiting for their release on God's Command...and the global judgment that follows.


**Revelation 9:14-15**
> “Saying to the sixth angel which had the trumpet, Loose the four angels which are bound in the great river Euphrates. And the four angels were loosed, which were prepared for an hour, and a day, and a month, and a year, to slay the third part of men.”


- This isn't metaphor and this isn't symbolic poetry. 
- This is a literal, prophetic event, scheduled in the sovereign calendar of Almighty God.

## Who Are These Angels?

- The honest answer is. I don't know. 
- The Bible gives no names. But what it does say speaks volumes.They are bound and chained.
- You see, in Scripture, only rebellious angels — fallen angels — are ever chained.
- Faithful angels are free, obeying God without hindrance. But these four are not free. They are imprisoned. Not in hell…not in a pit…but at the Euphrates River.


**2 Peter 2:4**
> “For if God did not spare the angels who sinned, but cast them down to hell and delivered them into chains of darkness…”


**Jude 1:6**
> “And the angels who did not keep their proper domain… He has reserved in everlasting chains under darkness for the judgment of the great day.”

These verses reveal the severity of their sin and the intensity of their punishment. Their power is so great — so dangerous — that God has kept them bound, locked away, until the precise moment they are to be used in His Judgment. 

## Why the Euphrates River?

- The Euphrates River is no random location.
- It’s one of the first rivers mentioned in Eden. It’s the cradle of human civilization — Babylon, Assyria, and ancient Mesopotamia.

**Genesis 2:13-15**
> The name of the second river is the Gihon; it winds through the entire land of Cush. 14 The name of the third river is the Tigris; it runs along the east side of Ashur. And the fourth river is the Euphrates. 15 The Lord God took the man and put him in the Garden of Eden to work it and take care of it.

- In Genesis, a single river flows out of the Garden of Eden and splits into four rivers: Pishon, Gihon, Tigris (Hiddekel), and Euphrates. 
- While the Tigris and Euphrates are well-known rivers in the Middle East, the locations of Pishon and Gihon are uncertain, with theories placing them in regions like Arabia or Ethiopia.
- The four rivers are believed to symbolize God’s provision, the interconnectedness of creation, and the spread of divine blessing — a theme echoed in the Christian message of sharing the gospel with the world.
- But spiritually, it represents the border between God's Kingdom and the forces of darkness. In prophecy, it is a boundary — a place where evil is restrained until God’s appointed time.
- You see, it’s no coincidence that the four most destructive angelic beings ever mentioned are being held there. The Euphrates becomes not just a place — it becomes a divine prison. A spiritual border.


## What Happens When They're Released?

- Scripture tells us exactly what they will do: “To kill a third of mankind.”

**Revelation 9:13-16**
> The sixth angel sounded his trumpet, and I heard a voice coming from the four horns of the golden altar that is before God. 14 It said to the sixth angel who had the trumpet, “Release the four angels who are bound at the great river Euphrates.” 15 And the four angels who had been kept ready for this very hour and day and month and year were released to kill a third of mankind. 16 The number of the mounted troops was twice ten thousand times ten thousand. I heard their number.

- Let that settle into your mind and heart:  One-third of humanity. 
- This is not just chaos or confusion. This is judgment. 
- These beings are not acting on their own. They are not random demons wreaking havoc. 
- They are instruments of divine wrath, released at a specific time, with a terrifying mission.
- Their actions will be global, violent, and devastating — affecting the political, spiritual, and social climate of the entire world. 

## Why Are They Still Bound?

- Because God is merciful. 
- Their imprisonment is not just about punishment. It’s about protection. 
- Right now, much evil is restrained compared to what is coming during the Great Tribulation. The full fury of darkness is held back by the hand of God.
- Their delayed release gives humanity more time to repent. But make no mistake: when they are unleashed, it will mark the beginning of one of the darkest chapters in human history — the Great Tribulation.
