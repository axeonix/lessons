- Christ will be sitting on the seat of Judgement, and he will honor the people with crowns. However, there are five Crowns in the Bible which will be given to the people who will do the work of God. The five crowns in the Bible and names for them in the Bible are:

## Crown # 1 The Incorruptible crown

The first crown is the Incorruptible crown in the Bible. It will be given to those who will have victory over the world and the flesh and the Satan's deceptions.

**Corinthians 9:25-27**
> "Everyone who competes in the games goes into strict training. They do it to get a crown that will not last, but we do it to get a crown that will last forever. Therefore I do not run like someone running aimlessly; I do not fight like a boxer beating the air. No, I strike a blow to my body and make it my slave so that after I have preached to others, I myself will not be disqualified for the prize."


## Crown # 2 The Crown of Rejoicing

The Second crown is the Crown of rejoicing, and it will be given to those who will win the souls for Christ. 

**1 Thessalonians 2:19**
> “You are our hope, our joy and the crown of rejoicing. We will be proud when our Lord Jesus Christ comes.”


## Crown # 3 The Crown of Eternal Life

This Crown will be given to those who will endure pain and be faithful in God in short for the martyrs.

**James 1:12**
> “Great blessings belong to those who are tempted and remain faithful! After they have proved their faith, God will give them the crown of eternal life.

**Revelation 2:10**
> “Do not fear any of those things which you are about to suffer. Indeed, the devil is about to put some of you into prison, that he will do this to test you, and you will suffer for ten days. But be faithful until death, and I will give you the crown of life.


## Crown # 4 The Crown of Glory

The fourth crown is the crown of glory, and it will be given to the shepherds. 

**1 Peter 5:4**
> “And when the chief Shepherd appears that you will receive the Crown of Glory that does not fade away.”


## Crown # 5 The Crown of righteousness

The fifth crown is the crown of righteousness, and it will be given to those who have the Hope for the second coming of Christ.

**2 Timothy 4:8**
> “Say’s now the prize is waiting for me, the crown that is right with God. The Lord, the righteous judge, will be giving it to me on that day. And not to me only, but also to everyone else who is eagerly looking forward to his coming.

**Revelation 22:12**
> “Look, I am coming soon! My reward is with me, and I will give to each person according to what they have done.

Don’t we all get the same rewards in Heaven? The short answer to this is no. The rewards that saints receive in Heaven will vary greatly. Some people think the term “eternal reward” just means that all saved people will be given the reward of eternal life.

The Bible promises several types of rewards to people who qualify for them. Here are some examples of crowns that the Word of God promises people: THE SOUL-WINNER’S CROWN – to people who labored to save souls from the fires of hell. 

**1 Thessalonians 2:19**
> “You are our hope, our joy, and the crown we will take pride in when our Lord Jesus Christ comes.”


Is it right for Christians to seek eternal rewards? Yes, it is.

First, we must determine what earns eternal rewards. The answer to this is wrapped up in the words of Jesus: 

**Matthew 6:33**
> “But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you”

The “things” that will be added when a Christian seeks to do what the Kingdom of God requires – absolute obedience to all commands found within His Word are blessings on this side of Heaven and are rewards for eternity.

Under this prescription found in **Matthew 6:33**, it is not only okay to desire and seek eternal rewards, but it is commanded by the believer.

Paul the Apostle made no apologies for saying:
**2 Timothy 4:7-8** 
> “I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith: Henceforth there is laid up for me a crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous judge, shall give me at that day: and not to me only, but unto all them also that love his appearing”

- We must make sure our desires are not selfish inward turning, so that it is all about us. We, as God’s children, are to seek with all our hearts to do what He put us here to do. Then, our heavenly father will see to it we are blessed beyond our wildest imaginations.
- This judgment will not be a judgment for sin. Everyone who appears at this Judgment Seat will be saved. Jesus took all sin upon himself on the cross, past, present and future.

**1 Peter 5:2-4**
> "Be shepherds of God’s flock that is under your care, watching over them — not because you must, but because you are willing, as God wants you to be; not pursuing dishonest gain, but eager to serve; not lording it over those entrusted to you, but being examples to the flock. And when the Chief Shepherd appears, you will receive the crown of glory that will never fade away."
