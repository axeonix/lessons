- *Health and Sanitation*: The restrictions protected Israel from disease by avoiding animals that carried parasites or toxins and by enforcing carcass-handling rules. Without medical knowledge, God preserved them physically.
- *Separation and Identity*: The dietary laws distinguished Israel from the surrounding nations and served as a daily reminder that they belonged to God. Every meal reinforced their covenant identity and holiness.
- *Spiritual and Moral Teaching*: Clean and unclean categories taught spiritual truths — symbolizing purity vs. impurity, life vs. death, and obedience vs. disobedience. The laws trained Israel to understand holiness in everyday life. 
- *New Testament Connection*: Jesus declared all foods clean (**Mark 7**), Peter’s vision abolished food boundaries (**Acts 10**), and Paul taught that food does not make one spiritually impure (**Romans 14**). The symbolic function was fulfilled in Christ, making holiness an inner transformation rather than a dietary system. Yet believers are still called to live set apart.
