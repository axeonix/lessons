## Point 1: Run the Race to Win the Prize

**1 Corinthians 9:24**
> “Do you not know that in a race all the runners run, but only one gets the prize? Run in such a way as to get the prize.”

- Paul says all the runners run, but only one gets the prize.
- It is not enough to be in the race. **Run in such a way as to win.**

**Romans 12:4-5**
> “For just as each of us has one body with many members, and these members do not all have the same function, 5 so in Christ we, though many, form one body, and each member belongs to all the others.”

- We are many members, but one body in Christ. Each member has its own function.
- Each of us has our own race to run. God gave you a part, and He expects you to run it faithfully.

**1 Corinthians 9:25-27**
> “Everyone who competes in the games goes into strict training. They do it to get a crown that will not last, but we do it to get a crown that will last forever. 26 Therefore I do not run like someone running aimlessly; I do not fight like a boxer beating the air. 27 No, I strike a blow to my body and make it my slave so that after I have preached to others, I myself will not be disqualified for the prize.”

- The world trains hard for a crown that will not last. We train for **a crown that will last forever**.
- Paul did not run aimlessly. He disciplined himself so that he would not be **disqualified for the prize**.

**Hebrews 12:1-2**
> “Therefore, since we are surrounded by such a great cloud of witnesses, let us throw off everything that hinders and the sin that so easily entangles. And let us run with perseverance the race marked out for us, 2 fixing our eyes on Jesus, the pioneer and perfecter of faith. For the joy set before him he endured the cross, scorning its shame, and sat down at the right hand of the throne of God.”

- To win the prize, throw off everything that hinders you and the sin that so easily entangles you.
- Run with perseverance, and keep your eyes fixed on Jesus. He finished His race, and He is our example.

**Revelation 22:12**
> “Look, I am coming soon! My reward is with me, and I will give to each person according to what they have done.”

- Jesus is coming soon, and **His reward is with Him**.
- The prize is given according to what we have done. How you run your race matters for eternity.

---

## Point 2: There Is Only One Gate

**Matthew 7:14**
> “But small is the gate and narrow the road that leads to life, and only a few find it.”

- There is one small gate, and His Name is Jesus Christ of Nazareth.
- If you try to go through any other gate, you cannot enter the Kingdom of Heaven. You will not be saved.
- There is no hope for you. There is no gate except for Christ.
- All of those who miss Christ miss forgiveness. They miss right standing with God, and they enter into a devil’s hell.

**Acts 4:12**
> “Salvation is found in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given to mankind by which we must be saved.”

**Matthew 7:13**
> “Enter through the narrow gate. For wide is the gate and broad is the road that leads to destruction, and many enter through it.”

---

## Point 3: Be Like the Bereans

**Acts 17:11**
> “Now the Berean Jews were of more noble character than those in Thessalonica, for they received the message with great eagerness and examined the Scriptures every day to see if what Paul said was true.”

- Question: What do we learn from the Bereans here?
- Many people who profess to be Christians think they can just check some boxes they were taught.
- Maybe they learned them at a church or a Bible discussion. Or maybe they are trusting their pastors.
- We need to be like the Bereans in the Book of Acts.
- They would listen to what Paul taught them. But then they would go and verify that what Paul taught was Biblically accurate.

**Matthew 7:21**
> “Not everyone who says to me, ‘Lord, Lord,’ will enter the kingdom of heaven, but only the one who does the will of my Father who is in heaven.”

- The vast majority of people, both outside the church and inside the church, will proclaim that they have passed through the small gate and believed in Jesus Christ.
- But when you look at their lives, they live just like the world. They have the same desires as the world.
- The only thing they do differently is proclaim they are religious and go to church on Sunday.
- But once they get to Monday and the new week, it is back to the world, and there is no Christ.
- When you look at their conversations, their desires, their dreams, and their passions, there is no Christ.

**1 Corinthians 1:18**
> “For the message of the cross is foolishness to those who are perishing, but to us who are being saved it is the power of God.”

- When asked about the confidence of their salvation, they say: “I prayed that prayer.”
- They are trusting their eternity to a prayer.

**Hebrews 12:8**
> “If you are not disciplined—and everyone undergoes discipline—then you are not legitimate, not true sons and daughters at all.”

- They have a little bit of religion, and they go to church on Sundays.
- They are not passionate about the Word of God. They are not passionate about knowing Jesus Christ.
- They are not convicted of sin. They never weep over the sin in their lives.
- They are never concerned about genuine fellowship with other believers.
- They are in church every Sunday, and they are pretty moral.
- But they do not grow in the things of God, and they have no passion for getting to know God better.
- This is so dangerous. And sadly, these are the type of people who fill up the church.

**1 John 1:6**
> “If we claim to have fellowship with him and yet walk in the darkness, we lie and do not live out the truth.”

## Point 4: Salvation Is a Supernatural Work of God

- **Salvation is a supernatural work of God.**
- If your heart has been transformed by the Holy Spirit, you will see that your lifestyle will be transformed.
- You will never be perfect. Only Jesus is.
- But He wants you to come as you are, completely broken and imperfect. He will fix you and transform you along the way.

**Matthew 7:16**
> “By their fruit you will recognize them. Do people pick grapes from thornbushes, or figs from thistles?”

**Ezekiel 36:26**
> “I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit in you; I will remove from you your heart of stone and give you a heart of flesh.”

## Point 5: The Day and Hour Unknown

**Matthew 24:36-51**
> ***The Day and Hour Unknown***
>
> <sup>36</sup> “But about that day or hour no one knows, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, but only the Father. <sup>37</sup> As it was in the days of Noah, so it will be at the coming of the Son of Man. <sup>38</sup> For in the days before the flood, people were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, up to the day Noah entered the ark; <sup>39</sup> and they knew nothing about what would happen until the flood came and took them all away. That is how it will be at the coming of the Son of Man. <sup>40</sup> Two men will be in the field; one will be taken and the other left. <sup>41</sup> Two women will be grinding with a hand mill; one will be taken and the other left.
>
> <sup>42</sup> “Therefore keep watch, because you do not know on what day your Lord will come. <sup>43</sup> But understand this: If the owner of the house had known at what time of night the thief was coming, he would have kept watch and would not have let his house be broken into. <sup>44</sup> So you also must be ready, because the Son of Man will come at an hour when you do not expect him.
>
> <sup>45</sup> “Who then is the faithful and wise servant, whom the master has put in charge of the servants in his household to give them their food at the proper time? <sup>46</sup> It will be good for that servant whose master finds him doing so when he returns. <sup>47</sup> Truly I tell you, he will put him in charge of all his possessions. <sup>48</sup> But suppose that servant is wicked and says to himself, ‘My master is staying away a long time,’ <sup>49</sup> and he then begins to beat his fellow servants and to eat and drink with drunkards. <sup>50</sup> The master of that servant will come on a day when he does not expect him and at an hour he is not aware of. <sup>51</sup> He will cut him to pieces and assign him a place with the hypocrites, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.

- Let us walk in the fear of the Lord. Let us walk in the holiness of God.
- God is faithful. God is willing that none shall perish.
- God does not want anyone to go to hell or to be beaten with any stripes.
- But God is holy. To profess His Name, the Name of Jesus Christ, and not depart from iniquity is deserving of stripes.
- That is why the Spirit of the Lord testifies who the children of God are.

**Romans 8:16**
> “The Spirit himself testifies with our spirit that we are God’s children.”

---

## Conclusion: Be Ready

- Our King is coming, and He is coming soon. So let us be ready at all times.
- Let us be watching for our Lord and Savior’s return, because time is running out.
- All of us need to be found on the side of God for all of eternity.
- Then we can worship Him in Spirit and in Truth, and love Him with everything that we are.

**Many are called, but few are chosen. Enter through the narrow gate.**
> *“For many are invited, but few are chosen.”* — Matthew 22:14

God Bless you all in The Mighty Name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.
