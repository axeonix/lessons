# Jacob’s Ladder: The Angels Are Watching – You Are Not Alone (Genesis 28:16)

**Opening Prayer**

## Introduction: You Might Want to Pay Attention to This

**Genesis 28:16**
> “When Jacob awoke from his sleep, he thought, ‘Surely the Lord is in this place, and I was not aware of it.’” (NIV)

- Have you ever gone through a season in life where you felt forgotten, even by God?
- Maybe you have asked: “Where is God in all of this?” Or: “Why me?”
- Today’s message reminds us that God is always present.
- He is present even when we cannot feel it, even when everything looks like it is falling apart.
- **He is with you. And His angels are watching.**
- Let us journey through the life of Jacob.
- Jacob was a man who ran, lied, tricked, and feared. And yet he was chosen by God. Why?
- God’s grace does not pick the most perfect. It chooses the most honest, the most broken, the most willing.

---

## Point 1: Jacob, the Unlikely Favorite

- Jacob’s story did not begin with favor. From the start, it seemed like he was in second place.

**Genesis 25:26**
> “After this, his brother came out, with his hand grasping Esau’s heel; so he was named Jacob.”

- Esau was Isaac’s favorite (**Genesis 25:28**). Jacob? Not so much.
- How many of us know what it feels like to be overlooked or overshadowed?
- How many of us have tried hard to please someone, only to never be enough?
- Later, with the encouragement of his mother, Jacob tricks his father into blessing him instead of Esau (**Genesis 27**).
- He deceives his way into the promise.
- **The blessing was stolen, but the calling was still real.** God still had a plan.

---

## Point 2: The Escape and the Encounter

- Now Jacob is on the run. His brother wants to kill him.
- He has no family near, no help, and no comfort.
- Alone, tired, and afraid, he lays his head on a rock to sleep.
- **That is when heaven touches earth.**

**Genesis 28:12-15**
> <sup>12</sup> He had a dream in which he saw a stairway resting on the earth, with its top reaching to heaven, and the angels of God were ascending and descending on it. <sup>13</sup> There above it stood the Lord, and he said: “I am the Lord, the God of your father Abraham and the God of Isaac. I will give you and your descendants the land on which you are lying. <sup>14</sup> Your descendants will be like the dust of the earth, and you will spread out to the west and to the east, to the north and to the south. All peoples on earth will be blessed through you and your offspring. <sup>15</sup> I am with you and will watch over you wherever you go, and I will bring you back to this land. I will not leave you until I have done what I have promised you.”

- Notice this: **Jacob did not climb the ladder. God came to him.**
- Jacob did nothing to deserve this encounter, but heaven came down anyway. That is grace.
- And God speaks directly to him, not just about blessings, but about His presence:
- **“I am with you and will watch over you wherever you go… I will not leave you until I have done what I have promised you”** (v. 15).

---

## Point 3: When God Opens Our Eyes

- Jacob wakes up shocked: **“Surely the Lord is in this place, and I was not aware of it”** (**Genesis 28:16**).
- Sometimes we do not realize God is with us until the trial has passed.
- Sometimes we miss His presence in the pain.
- But God was there. The angels were already ascending and descending.
- Let me ask you:
  * Have you missed God in the middle of your storm?
  * Are you only seeing your struggle, and forgetting your Source?

### Elisha and His Servant: Seeing Beyond the Visible

- Another story brings this home.
- In **2 Kings 6**, Elisha’s servant was terrified when he saw an enemy army surrounding them.
- But Elisha prayed.

**2 Kings 6:17**
> “And Elisha prayed, ‘Open his eyes, Lord, so that he may see.’ Then the Lord opened the servant’s eyes, and he looked and saw the hills full of horses and chariots of fire all around Elisha.”

- Here is the truth: **what you see is not all there is.**
- There are angels watching. There is a spiritual realm fighting on your behalf.
- **You may feel surrounded, but you are not defeated.**

---

## Point 4: God’s Presence Is the Promise

- When Jacob saw that ladder, he learned something we must never forget: **God is with us.**
  * Not just in church, but in the wilderness.
  * Not just on Sundays, but in the silence.
  * Not just in blessings, but in battles.
- That is why God says:

**Deuteronomy 31:6**
> “Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid or terrified because of them, for the Lord your God goes with you; he will never leave you nor forsake you.”

- And again:

**Psalm 91:11**
> “For he will command his angels concerning you to guard you in all your ways.”

---

## Point 5: Do Not Just Seek Safety. Seek God.

- We are living in uncertain times.
- Wars, inflation, divisions, confusion, darkness…
- But our top prayer should not be: “Lord, fix the world.”
- It should be: **“Lord, help me know You. Open my eyes. Help me see that You are here.”**

**Matthew 22:37**
> “Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind.”

---

## Point 6: You Have Been Protected More Than You Know

- I have walked through dangerous places. Maybe you have too.
- I have come through storms that should have broken me. But here I am. Why?
- **Because the angels were watching.**
- Because the Lord was with me, and I did not even know it.
- Jacob said it best: **“Surely the Lord is in this place, and I was not aware of it.”**

---

## Conclusion: The Lord Is With You

- Let me leave you with this final truth: **God is with you.**
- He is ahead of you. Behind you. Inside you.
- He has not forgotten you.
- His angels are still on assignment concerning your life.

**Jeremiah 29:11**
> “For I know the plans I have for you,” declares the Lord, “plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future.”

**Psalm 34:7**
> “The angel of the Lord encamps around those who fear him, and he delivers them.”

**You are not alone. The Lord is in this place.**
> *“Surely the Lord is in this place, and I was not aware of it.”* — Genesis 28:16

God Bless you all, and may the Grace of Our Lord Jesus Christ be with you always. Amen and Amen.

**Pray for Communion**
