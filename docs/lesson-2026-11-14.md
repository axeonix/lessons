# The Books of Heaven: What Has God Written About Your Life?

**Opening Prayer**

## Introduction: God Keeps Records

- Have you ever stopped to consider that the Bible tells us God has books?
- Not just one book, but several books and heavenly records that are mentioned throughout Scripture.
- We read about the Book of Life, the Book of Remembrance, the Book of Truth, the Books of Judgment, and even a record of our tears.
- Think about that for a moment. God knows our names. He knows our thoughts.
- He knows our words, our actions, our struggles, and our faithfulness.
- He even knows the tears we cry when nobody else is watching.
- The Bible gives us a glimpse into the heavenly records of God.
- And one day, according to Scripture, **those books will be opened.**

**Revelation 20:12**
> “And I saw the dead, great and small, standing before the throne, and books were opened. Another book was opened, which is the book of life.”

- Notice that John says **books** were opened. And then **another book** was opened, which is the Book of Life.
- This is a sobering reminder that our lives matter to God, and nothing is hidden from Him.

**Hebrews 4:13**
> “Nothing in all creation is hidden from God’s sight. Everything is uncovered and laid bare before the eyes of him to whom we must give account.”

- Let us look at the books God has mentioned in the Bible.

---

## Point 1: The Book of Life

- The Book of Life is directly mentioned in **Philippians 4:3**, **Revelation 3:5**, **Revelation 20:12, 15**, and **Revelation 21:27**.
- This book contains the names of those who belong to God and have eternal life.

**Philippians 4:3**
> “Yes, and I ask you, my true companion, help these women since they have contended at my side in the cause of the gospel, along with Clement and the rest of my co-workers, whose names are in the book of life.”

- Paul encourages believers to help Euodia and Syntyche settle their differences.
- Both women had faithfully worked alongside him in spreading the Gospel.
- He also recognizes Clement and other fellow workers whose names are written in the Book of Life.
- The central message: God values those who faithfully serve Him. And disagreements should never divide those who are working together for His Kingdom.
- This verse teaches us three important lessons:
  * **Unity matters.** Believers should put aside personal disagreements and work together in love.
  * **Faithful service matters.** God recognizes and remembers those who labor for the Gospel.
  * **Eternal life matters.** Being recorded in the Book of Life is far greater than earthly recognition. It points to our eternal relationship with God.
- We may disagree with one another. But we should never allow disagreements to become greater than our commitment to Christ.

**Revelation 3:5**
> “The one who is victorious will, like them, be dressed in white. I will never blot out the name of that person from the book of life, but will acknowledge that name before my Father and his angels.”

**Revelation 20:15**
> “Anyone whose name was not found written in the book of life was thrown into the lake of fire.”

**Revelation 21:27**
> “Nothing impure will ever enter it, nor will anyone who does what is shameful or deceitful, but only those whose names are written in the Lamb’s book of life.”

- The Book of Life is perhaps the most important book mentioned in Scripture.
- Think about the importance of this.
- You can have your name written on a birth certificate, a marriage certificate, a business, or a house.
- You can even have it on a famous building, or on the sidewalk in Hollywood on the Walk of Fame.
- But the question that matters for eternity is this: **Is your name written in the Book of Life?**
- If you have confessed that Jesus is Lord, have been baptized in His name, and have repented of your sins, then your name is written there.
- But here is the key to it all. **It all comes down to love.**
- If you love God, if you love Jesus, then obey His commands.

**John 14:15**
> “If you love me, keep my commands.”

- Notice the first word in that Scripture: **“If.”**
- “If” is a word that introduces a condition. It points to something that may or may not happen, like “in the event that.”
- It can also express uncertainty.
- Our greatest concern should not be how well known we are here on earth, but whether we belong to Jesus Christ.

---

## Point 2: The Book of Remembrance: God Remembers Your Faithfulness

- This is one of my favorite passages.

**Malachi 3:16**
> “Then those who feared the LORD talked with each other, and the LORD listened and heard. A scroll of remembrance was written in his presence concerning those who feared the LORD and honored his name.”

- What a beautiful thought!
- God was listening when His people talked about Him. He noticed those who honored His name. He remembered their faithfulness.
- Sometimes we wonder whether our prayers, our service, our sacrifices, and our efforts to encourage others make a difference.
  * Perhaps nobody else notices what you do for God.
  * Perhaps you serve quietly.
  * Perhaps you encourage someone who is struggling.
  * Perhaps you pray for people who will never know you prayed for them.
- **But God knows.**
- You may be forgotten by people, but **you are not forgotten by God.**
- God hears the conversations of those who fear Him, honor Him, and stay faithful to Him.
- He listens when believers encourage one another, and He keeps a scroll of remembrance in His presence.
- God never forgets those who love Him, respect Him, and stay faithful to His Word.

---

## Point 3: The Book of God’s Decrees (The Book of Destiny)

**Psalm 139:16**
> Your eyes saw my unformed body;<br>
> &emsp;&emsp;all the days ordained for me were written in your book<br>
> &emsp;&emsp;before one of them came to be.

- This passage describes God’s knowledge of our lives before we were born.
- It is sometimes called the Book of Destiny. But that is a descriptive title, not the Bible’s actual name for it.

---

## Point 4: The Books of Judgment (The Books of Deeds or Works)

**Daniel 7:9-10**
> <sup>9</sup> “As I looked, thrones were set in place, and the Ancient of Days took his seat. His clothing was as white as snow; the hair of his head was white like wool. His throne was flaming with fire, and its wheels were all ablaze. <sup>10</sup> A river of fire was flowing, coming out from before him. Thousands upon thousands attended him; ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him. The court was seated, and the books were opened.”

### The Great White Throne Judgment

**Revelation 20:12-13**
> <sup>12</sup> “And I saw the dead, great and small, standing before the throne, and books were opened. Another book was opened, which is the book of life. The dead were judged according to what they had done as recorded in the books. <sup>13</sup> The sea gave up the dead that were in it, and death and Hades gave up the dead that were in them, and each person was judged according to what they had done.”

- These books contain records of people’s deeds.
- Revelation says the dead were judged according to what was recorded in the books.
- Here is an important difference:
  * Revelation names **one separate book** as the Book of Life.
  * The **other books** contain the records by which people are judged.

---

## Point 5: God’s Book (The Book of the Living)

**Exodus 32:32-33**
> <sup>32</sup> “But now, please forgive their sin—but if not, then blot me out of the book you have written.” <sup>33</sup> The LORD replied to Moses, “Whoever has sinned against me I will blot out of my book.”

- When Israel sinned by worshiping the golden calf, Moses pleaded with God to forgive the people.
- He was willing to be blotted out of God’s book for their sake.
- God answered that whoever had sinned against Him would be blotted out of His book.

**Psalm 69:28**
> May they be blotted out of the book of life<br>
> &emsp;&emsp;and not be listed with the righteous.

- This appears to describe a divine register of those who are alive.
- It is not necessarily a separate book from the Book of Life.

---

## Point 6: The Book of Tears

- In **Psalm 56**, David writes about his suffering, and how God sees every tear he sheds.

**Psalm 56:8**
> Record my misery;<br>
> &emsp;&emsp;list my tears on your scroll—<br>
> &emsp;&emsp;are they not in your record?

- What a powerful picture of God’s compassion!
- God does not overlook our suffering, our sorrow, or the tears we cry when nobody else sees them.

---

## Point 7: The Book of Truth

**Daniel 10:21**
> “But first I will tell you what is written in the Book of Truth. (No one supports me against them except Michael, your prince.)”

- The angel speaking to Daniel refers to the Book of Truth.
- This passage refers to God’s revealed purposes for future events and the kingdoms of the world.
- It is not described as a heavenly record of people’s names or deeds.
- But it is an intriguing reference to a book connected with divine truth and God’s purposes.

---

## Point 8: The Book of the Lamb

**Revelation 13:8**
> “All inhabitants of the earth will worship the beast—all whose names have not been written in the Lamb’s book of life, the Lamb who was slain from the creation of the world.”

- **Revelation 21:27** calls it: **“the Lamb’s book of life.”**
- This is not necessarily a separate book from the Book of Life.
- It is the Book of Life belonging to Jesus Christ, the Lamb of God. It records those who belong to Him.

---

## Point 9: The Book with Seven Seals

**Revelation 5:1-9**
> <sup>1</sup> Then I saw in the right hand of him who sat on the throne a scroll with writing on both sides and sealed with seven seals. <sup>2</sup> And I saw a mighty angel proclaiming in a loud voice, “Who is worthy to break the seals and open the scroll?” <sup>3</sup> But no one in heaven or on earth or under the earth could open the scroll or even look inside it. <sup>4</sup> I wept and wept because no one was found who was worthy to open the scroll or look inside. <sup>5</sup> Then one of the elders said to me, “Do not weep! See, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has triumphed. He is able to open the scroll and its seven seals.”
>
> <sup>6</sup> Then I saw a Lamb, looking as if it had been slain, standing at the center of the throne, encircled by the four living creatures and the elders. The Lamb had seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven spirits of God sent out into all the earth. <sup>7</sup> He went and took the scroll from the right hand of him who sat on the throne. <sup>8</sup> And when he had taken it, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb. Each one had a harp and they were holding golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of God’s people. <sup>9</sup> And they sang a new song, saying: “You are worthy to take the scroll and to open its seals, because you were slain, and with your blood you purchased for God persons from every tribe and language and people and nation.”

- No one in heaven or on earth was found worthy to open the scroll. John wept.
- **Only the Lamb who was slain, Jesus Christ, is worthy to open it.**

---

## Point 10: More Than the World Could Contain

**John 21:25**
> “Jesus did many other things as well. If every one of them were written down, I suppose that even the whole world would not have room for the books that would be written.”

- Jesus did far more miracles, taught far more lessons, and did far more acts of love and compassion than could ever be recorded.
- His earthly ministry was so extraordinary that the world itself could not hold all the books describing everything He did.
  * **The greatness of Jesus:** His works and teachings were beyond anything that could be fully written down.
  * **The Gospel accounts:** The Bible records selected events that reveal who Jesus is and lead people to believe in Him.
  * **Faith and awe:** Jesus’ ministry reminds us of the immeasurable greatness of God’s grace.
- There is always more to learn about Christ, His character, His power, and His love.
- Even if we had 10,000 years to go through the Holy Bible, we could not talk enough about all the great things God and Jesus have done, and are still doing.
- The good news is that Jesus told us He is coming soon, and **He never breaks a promise.**

**Revelation 22:12**
> “Look, I am coming soon! My reward is with me, and I will give to each person according to what they have done.”

---

## Conclusion: Is Your Name Written There?

- The Bible gives us enough to know Jesus, believe in Him, and receive eternal life.
- Yet His works and greatness are far beyond what human words could ever fully describe.
- God keeps a record of those who fear Him, honor His name, and speak with one another about Him.
- Our names being written in the Book of Life is far more important than being recognized by the world.
- **Stay connected to God.** Read His Word daily. Pray to Him, and He will listen to you.

**What has God written about your life?**
> *“Another book was opened, which is the book of life.”* — Revelation 20:12

God Bless you all in The Mighty Name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth. Amen and Hallelujah.
