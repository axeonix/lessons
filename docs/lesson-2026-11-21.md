# Growing Together, Not Apart (Ecclesiastes 4:9-12)

**Opening Prayer**

## Introduction

- God designed marriage so that two people would grow stronger together.
- He did not design it for two people to slowly drift into separate lives under the same roof.
- Many couples start marriage with great hopes… and then years later find themselves living parallel lives.
- Same house, same kids, same calendar. Yet emotionally, spiritually, and relationally, they are miles apart.
- God never intended marriage to be two people sharing space while growing in opposite directions.
- **His design is that we grow toward each other and, together, toward Him.**

**Ecclesiastes 4:9-12**
> <sup>9</sup> Two are better than one,<br>
> &emsp;&emsp;because they have a good return for their labor:<br>
> <sup>10</sup> If either of them falls down,<br>
> &emsp;&emsp;one can help the other up.<br>
> But pity anyone who falls<br>
> &emsp;&emsp;and has no one to help them up.<br>
> <sup>11</sup> Also, if two lie down together, they will keep warm.<br>
> &emsp;&emsp;But how can one keep warm alone?<br>
> <sup>12</sup> Though one may be overpowered,<br>
> &emsp;&emsp;two can defend themselves.<br>
> A cord of three strands is not quickly broken.

- Notice the progression: two are better than one…
- And the strongest version of “two” includes a third strand, **which is God Himself.**

---

## Point 1: Growing Together Requires Leaving and Cleaving

**Genesis 2:24**
> “That is why a man leaves his father and mother and is united to his wife, and they become one flesh.”

- Leaving is more than geography. **It means transferring your first loyalty.**
- Cleaving means being glued together. It means choosing oneness over independence, on purpose.
- Some couples keep one foot in their family of origin, their old friendships, or their personal independence.
- When they do, they slowly grow apart.
- Ask yourself: **What, or who, still has a stronger claim on your loyalty than your spouse?**

---

## Point 2: Growing Together Requires Daily, Practical Love

**John 15:13**
> “Greater love has no one than this: to lay down one’s life for one’s friends.”

**Ephesians 5:25**
> “Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her.”

**1 Corinthians 13:4-5**
> “Love is patient, love is kind. It does not envy, it does not boast, it is not proud. It does not dishonor others, it is not self-seeking, it is not easily angered, it keeps no record of wrongs.”

- Love is not mainly a feeling. Nor is it a “second-hand emotion,” as Tina Turner’s song says.
- **Love is a series of daily choices.**
- Growing apart usually happens through small, repeated acts of neglect, sarcasm, busyness, or keeping score.
- Growing together happens through small, repeated acts of patience, kindness, and forgiveness.
- Ask yourself:
  * Am I more concerned with being understood, or with understanding?
  * Am I more focused on my rights, or on serving?

---

## Point 3: Growing Together Requires Mutual Submission and Putting the Other First

**Philippians 2:3-4**
> “Do nothing out of selfish ambition or vain conceit. Rather, in humility value others above yourselves, not looking to your own interests but each of you to the interests of the others.”

**Ephesians 5:21**
> “Submit to one another out of reverence for Christ.”

- The opposite of growing together is self-centered living.
- Healthy marriages are not 50/50. **They are 100/100,** each person laying down their life for the other.

---

## Point 4: Growing Together Requires the Third Strand, Which Is God

- **“A cord of three strands is not quickly broken.”**

**Colossians 3:12-14**
> “Therefore, as God’s chosen people, holy and dearly loved, clothe yourselves with compassion, kindness, humility, gentleness and patience. Bear with each other and forgive one another if any of you has a grievance against someone. Forgive as the Lord forgave you. And over all these virtues put on love, which binds them all together in perfect unity.”

- Two people trying to hold a marriage together by willpower alone will eventually fray.
- When both spouses are pursuing Christ, they are pulled toward the same center, and so toward each other.
- Growing apart is usually gradual and quiet.
- **Growing together is intentional, and often costly.**

### Practical Next Steps

- **Pray together**, even 5 minutes a day.
- **Schedule regular, phone-free time** to talk about more than schedules and errands.
- **Ask forgiveness** for the ways you have added to the distance.
- **Re-commit to the covenant.**

**Matthew 19:6**
> “What God has joined together, let no one separate.”

- Marriage is not meant to be two people slowly becoming strangers.
- **It is meant to be two people becoming more one, and more like Christ, with every passing year.**

## Point 6: The Responsibilities of a Husband

### First: Love One Another

- The first and foremost responsibility of both husband and wife in marriage is this: **love one another.**
- This is a great commandment from God our Father.
- It is not an option. It is not a recommendation. **It is a commandment.**
- **Ephesians 5:25** teaches us: “Husbands, love your wives…”
- If you do not love your wife, you are disobeying Scripture.
- How much? “…just as Christ loved the church and gave himself up for her.”
- Notice what kind of love this is. **It is a self-giving love.** Not a taking love, but a giving love.

**John 13:34-35**
> <sup>34</sup> “A new command I give you: Love one another. As I have loved you, so you must love one another. <sup>35</sup> By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you love one another.”

**Matthew 22:36-40**
> <sup>36</sup> “Teacher, which is the greatest commandment in the Law?” <sup>37</sup> Jesus replied: “‘Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind.’ <sup>38</sup> This is the first and greatest commandment. <sup>39</sup> And the second is like it: ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’ <sup>40</sup> All the Law and the Prophets hang on these two commandments.”

### Second: Be the Head

**1 Corinthians 11:3**
> “But I want you to realize that the head of every man is Christ, and the head of the woman is man, and the head of Christ is God.”

- What are the functions of a head?
- If you think about the natural, physical body, I would suggest four things:
  * The head **receives input** from the whole body.
  * The head **makes decisions.**
  * The head **starts action.**
  * The head **gives ongoing direction.**
- Those are the responsibilities of a man.

### Third: Provide

**1 Timothy 5:8**
> “If anyone does not provide for his own, and especially for those of his household, he has denied the faith and is worse than an unbeliever.”

- There are even preachers out there who go around preaching but are not providing for their own households.
- The Bible says they are worse than an unbeliever.
- There are many preachers, life coaches, and counselors working in somebody else’s territory, but not in their own homes.

### Fourth: Wash Your Family with the Word

**Ephesians 5:25-26**
> “Christ… gave himself for the church, that he might sanctify and cleanse it with the washing of water by the word.”

- I believe that is a husband’s responsibility.
- **It is to wash his wife and his family with the teaching of the Word of God.**
- I believe that has a cleansing, sanctifying effect that nothing else can ever have.
- That does not mean the wife cannot preach the Word of God.
- But the primary responsibility, the source of teaching in the home, should be the husband.

### Fifth: Nourish and Cherish

**Ephesians 5:29**
> “For no one ever hated his own flesh, but nourishes and cherishes it, just as the Lord does the church.”

- So husbands, you have a responsibility to **nourish and cherish** your wives.

---

## Conclusion: No Perfect Husbands or Wives

- We all have lessons in our lives that we need to learn from. I have tried to learn mine.
- There are no perfect husbands or brothers, wives or sisters.
- Even preachers are not perfect husbands. In fact, to tell the truth, sometimes they are some of the worst.
- But God calls every one of us to grow together, not apart.

**A cord of three strands is not quickly broken.**
> *“What God has joined together, let no one separate.”* — Matthew 19:6

God Bless you all in The Mighty Name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth.
