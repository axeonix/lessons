## Point 1: The World Does Not End. The Age of Grace Does.

- When will the world end? Many people ask this and try to predict it.
- But the truth is this: **the world does not end.**
- Why would Jesus say the meek will inherit the earth if the world has ended?

**Matthew 5:5**
> “Blessed are the meek, for they will inherit the earth.”

**Revelation 21:5**
> “He who was seated on the throne said, ‘I am making everything new!’ Then he said, ‘Write this down, for these words are trustworthy and true.’”

- This is a promise of renewal and restoration.
- It shows God’s plan to create a new heaven and a new earth, free from sin and suffering.
- The world does not end, yet God does say He makes it new.
- Do you want to know what ends? **God’s grace.**
- That is what comes to an end at some point, and it is called **the end of the age.**

**Matthew 28:18-20**
> <sup>18</sup> Then Jesus came to them and said, “All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. <sup>19</sup> Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, <sup>20</sup> and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you. And surely I am with you always, to the very end of the age.”

**Isaiah 55:6**
> Seek the LORD while he may be found;<br>
> &emsp;&emsp;call on him while he is near.

- There will be a time when He will not be found.
- **Do it now,** or be ready to represent yourself in front of the Great White Throne.

## Point 2: The Great White Throne Judgment

- The Great White Throne Judgment is described in **Revelation 20:11-15.**
- It is the final judgment, before the lost are cast into the lake of fire.
- From **Revelation 20:7-15**, we know this judgment takes place after the millennium.
- It comes after Satan is thrown into the lake of fire, where the beast and the false prophet are (**Revelation 19:19-20; 20:7-10**).
- The books that are opened (**Revelation 20:12**) contain records of everyone’s deeds, good or evil.
- God knows everything that has ever been said, done, or even thought.
- And He will reward or punish each one accordingly (**Psalm 28:4; 62:12; Romans 2:6; Revelation 2:23; 18:6; 22:12**).
- At this time, another book is opened, called the **Book of Life** (**Revelation 20:12**).
- It is this book that decides whether a person will inherit eternal life with God, or receive everlasting punishment in the lake of fire.
- Christians are held accountable for their actions. But they are forgiven in Christ.
- Their names were written in the “book of life from the creation of the world” (**Revelation 17:8**).
- At this judgment, the dead will be “judged according to what they had done” (**Revelation 20:12**).
- And “anyone whose name was not found written in the book of life was thrown into the lake of fire” (**Revelation 20:15**).
- Scripture clearly confirms there will be a final judgment for all people, both believers and unbelievers.
- **Every person will one day stand before Christ and be judged for his or her deeds.**

## Point 3: The Signs of the End of the Age

- Every war, famine, and earthquake seems to make Christians think again that the end is near.
- We are right to think these things, because of Jesus’ prophetic words.

**Matthew 24:7-8**
> <sup>7</sup> “For nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom: and there shall be famines, and pestilences, and earthquakes, in diverse places. <sup>8</sup> All these are the beginning of sorrows.”

### We Are Living in the Last Days

- How close is the end of God’s grace? **Closer than you might think.**
- First, it is important to understand that we have been living in the last days for centuries now.
- On the day the Holy Spirit gave birth to the church, Peter explained it with a prophecy from the Old Testament.
- He said that we are now in the last days.

**Acts 2:17**
> “‘In the last days, God says, I will pour out my Spirit on all people. Your sons and daughters will prophesy, your young men will see visions, your old men will dream dreams.’”

- Look at the Old Testament story of Israel.
- God warned the Israelites for hundreds of years about the coming judgment on them as a nation.
- But He gave them plenty of time to repent and change their ways.
- In the end, judgment came at the hands of foreign nations.
- Now God is warning of judgment, not just on one nation, but on the whole planet and all people.
- So it makes sense that God would give mankind plenty of time to repent, even if that is thousands of years.
- But just because God has waited this long, we should not fool ourselves into thinking the end is far off.
- When the disciples asked Jesus about the end of the age in **Matthew 24**, He gave them signs. These signs are being fulfilled today.

### Sign: Persecution

**Matthew 24:9**
> “Then you will be handed over to be persecuted and put to death, and you will be hated by all nations because of me.”

- Jesus warned that before the end, His followers would be handed over to be persecuted and put to death.

### Sign: Hated by All Nations

- Right after warning about persecution, Jesus says: **“You will be hated by all nations because of me.”**
- This sign is harder to measure. There is no “hatred index” to tell us how much the world hates Christians.
- But we can look at the evidence in two parts of the world where Christianity used to be strong: **America and Europe.**
- For centuries, these two centers of civilization were the breadbasket of the Christian faith.
- Over the past several decades, they have become a wasteland of Christian support. Europe more so, with America close behind.
- Laws and regulations are stripping away Christian influence in places where we once flourished.
- In many nations, Christianity is no longer celebrated. Instead, it is suppressed.
- Jesus warned that before the end, we would be hated by all nations because of our loyalty to Him.

### Sign: The Love of Most Will Grow Cold

**Matthew 24:12**
> “Because of the increase of wickedness, the love of most will grow cold.”

- This one is also hard to measure. There is no scientific way to measure how hot or cold our love is.
- But we can sense an increase in wickedness all around us.
- Look at where our morals are as a nation. Look at our views on ethics, morality, and sexuality.
- We are living in a new moral age, one that is more and more wicked.
- Because of that, the love even of Christians has grown cold.
- Look at the lifeless, empty, dying churches that dot the landscape.
- Jesus warned that this would be a sign that the end is coming.

### Sign: The Gospel Preached to the Whole World

- The final sign Jesus gave His disciples was a positive one:

**Matthew 24:14**
> “And this gospel of the kingdom will be preached in the whole world as a testimony to all nations, and then the end will come.”

## Point 5: How Close Is the End of the Age?

- How close is the end of the age? **A lot closer than we might think.**
- About 2,000 years have passed since Jesus’ prophecy. Do not let that lull you into a false sense of comfort.
- Jesus told us His coming would be like a thief in the night.

**Matthew 24:4**
> “Jesus answered: ‘Watch out that no one deceives you.’”

## Point 6: Seared Consciences in the Later Times

**1 Timothy 4:1-2**
> <sup>1</sup> The Spirit clearly says that in later times some will abandon the faith and follow deceiving spirits and things taught by demons. <sup>2</sup> Such teachings come through hypocritical liars, whose consciences have been seared as with a hot iron.

- The Holy Spirit has revealed the marks of the end times. One of them is **seared consciences.**
- Remember my leg. A seared vein no longer carries blood. **A seared conscience no longer feels the warning of sin.**
- You cannot depart from the faith if you were never part of the faith.
- If you always belonged to the world, or were always an unbeliever, you cannot depart from the faith. You were never in it.
- This passage is speaking about those who are in the faith, but at some point they leave it.
- They leave because of two things: **deceiving spirits** and **things taught by demons.**
- As a result, they have seared consciences.

### The Battle Is in the Mind

- The devil is after the human conscience. **The battle takes place in our mind.**
- That is why God tells us to put on our spiritual armor.
- Nowhere in the Bible does God say He will put this armor on for us. **We have to put it on ourselves.**
- And the armor is the Word of God.

**Ephesians 6:10-20**
> <sup>10</sup> Finally, be strong in the Lord and in his mighty power. <sup>11</sup> Put on the full armor of God, so that **you** can take your stand against the devil’s schemes. <sup>12</sup> For our struggle is not against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the powers of this dark world and against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly realms. <sup>13</sup> Therefore put on the full armor of God, so that when the day of evil comes, **you** may be able to stand your ground, and after you have done everything, to stand. <sup>14</sup> Stand firm then, with the belt of truth buckled around your waist, with the breastplate of righteousness in place, <sup>15</sup> and with **your** feet fitted with the readiness that comes from the gospel of peace. <sup>16</sup> In addition to all this, take up the shield of faith, with which **you** can extinguish all the flaming arrows of the evil one. <sup>17</sup> Take the helmet of salvation and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.
>
> <sup>18</sup> And pray in the Spirit on all occasions with all kinds of prayers and requests. With this in mind, be alert and always keep on praying for all the Lord’s people. <sup>19</sup> Pray also for me, that whenever I speak, words may be given me so that I will fearlessly make known the mystery of the gospel, <sup>20</sup> for which I am an ambassador in chains. Pray that I may declare it fearlessly, as I should.

- Pay attention to **verse 17.** There is a reason God calls it **the helmet of salvation.**
- It is clear: the battlefield is in your mind, against the devil and his helpers.
- **Put on your armor and protect yourself.**
- If you are not armored up, you leave weak spots in your defense.
- Some temptations will be very hard to overcome if you are not fully armored up.
- We have to put on God’s armor **every day:**
  * the belt of truth,
  * the breastplate of righteousness,
  * the shoes of peace,
  * the helmet of salvation, on your head, because the battlefield is in our minds,
  * the shield of faith,
  * and the sword of the Spirit, which is God’s Word.

## Point 7: The Memory of Sin

- The human conscience is something we should not take lightly. The human memory is also something we should not take lightly.
- One thing I have seen is that people remember the sins they committed in the past.
- Decades go by, and they are still haunted by the memories of their sins.
- **This is another reason why you should avoid sin.**
- The memory of sins we have committed can come back and flash before our eyes. It can come out of nowhere.
- You could be having a moment of joy with your family, and suddenly a memory comes into your mind of a sin you committed years ago.
- It could even be a sin that God has already forgiven you for.

## Conclusion: Seek the Lord While He May Be Found

- The world will be made new, but the age of grace will come to an end.
- The signs Jesus gave are being fulfilled before our eyes.
- So guard your conscience. Avoid sin. **Armor up every day** with the Word of God.
- Seek the Lord now, while He may be found.

**Seek the Lord while He may be found.**
> *“Seek the LORD while he may be found; call on him while he is near.”* — Isaiah 55:6
