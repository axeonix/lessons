**Opening Prayer**

## Introduction

**Hebrews 9:27**
> “It is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment.” 

- In **Hebrews 9:27** we see that one day, we will stand before the Lord God Almighty. 
- Before the Lord who is the Author of our lives. The One who formed us in the womb. The One who knows everything about us — every thought, every action, every motive.
- Imagine standing in the presence of perfect holiness. 
- His eyes like flames of fire, piercing through every layer of your being. 
- Nothing hidden. Nothing overlooked. Every second of your life laid bare.
- I want you to just stop right now and imagine that moment. 

**Ecclesiastes 12:14**
> “For God will bring every deed into judgment, including every hidden thing, whether it is good or evil.” 

- What if in that moment, God said: “I gave you talents, gifts, and opportunities...but you never reached your full potential. 
- What if He said:  "I whispered to you — that still small voice — Telling you over and over again: There is more to life than this. But you ignored Me. You chose a life I never designed for you.”
- Some of you hearing this will never become the person God created you to be. 
- Not because He didn't give you the chance, But because you refused to answer the call.
- God has already placed potential inside you. 

I want to remind you that:
- You are not an accident and you are not here by chance.
- The odds of you even being born were 1 in 400 trillion and that doesn’t even consider the chances of your parents meeting.
- You have a better chance of being struck by lightning...than simply existing.
- Don’t dare believe the lie that you don’t matter and don’t ever say your life has no purpose. You are here because God chose you.

## Proving this through passages from God's Word:

### Nazia please read:

**Jeremiah 1:5** 
> “Before I formed you in the womb I knew you, before you were born I set you apart.”

- Here in Jeremiah we learn some amazing things about ourselves before we were even born
- We learn that:
  - God knew us before we were even formed in our mother's womb. 
  - He sanctified us. Meaning He set us apart BEFORE we were even born.
  - He ordained us...meaning that He called us to a life of purpose. 
- This is why we never felt like we fully fit in — because we were never meant to blend in. 
- We were called to stand out.

### Nazia please read:

**Exodus 9:16**

  - Every tree, every bird, every star in the sky…

- You were born with greatness in you. 
- You were born with heaven’s fingerprint on your soul. 
  - **Philippians 4:13**
    > “I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.” — 
  - This means:
    - Failure can't stop you.

### Nazia please read:

**1 Peter 2:9**  
> But you are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, God’s special possession, that you may declare the praises of him who called you out of darkness into his wonderful light.

- Here **1 Peter 2:9** describes believers as a "chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, and God’s special possession." 
- This verse emphasizes the unique identity and purpose of Christians
- They are set apart by God to reflect His glory and proclaim His greatness, having been brought out of darkness into His marvelous light.
  - **Chosen Generation**: Believers are intentionally selected by God.
  - **Royal Priesthood**: They hold both the honor of royalty and the role of priests — serving, worshiping, and having direct access to God.
  - **Holy Nation**: Christians form a distinct, sacred community set apart for God’s purposes.
  - **God’s Special Possession**: They are deeply valued and belong to God.

### Nazia please read:

**Ephesians 1:4-6**
> For he chose us in him before the creation of the world to be holy and blameless in his sight. In love He predestined us for adoption to sonship through Jesus Christ, in accordance with his pleasure and will — to the praise of his glorious grace, which he has freely given us in the One he loves.

- This verse teaches that God’s decision to call and love His people was made before the foundation of the world — long before we ever existed. 
- His choice was not based on our actions or merit, but was rooted in His eternal purpose and grace.
- **He chose us in Him**: God selected believers to be united with Christ. The choice is not apart from Christ, but entirely in Him — emphasizing that salvation is through and because of Jesus.
- **Before the creation of the world**: This points to the timeless nature of God's plan. His purpose was established in eternity, not in response to human history or behavior.
- **To be holy and blameless in His sight**: God's purpose in choosing us is not only salvation but sanctification. He calls His people to live holy lives that reflect His character, made possible through Christ.
- In LOVE He predestined us for adoption to sonship and daughtership through Jesus Christ
- This in accordance with his good pleasure and will

### Nazia please read:

**2 Timothy 1:9**
> “He has saved us and called us to a holy calling, not according to our works, but according to His own purpose and grace, which was given to us in Christ Jesus before time began.”

Here in **2 Timothy 1:9** the verse reinforces WHAT God’s call in our life is TRULY based on:
- It say here it is based on His purpose and grace
- It says it is NOT based on anything we’ve done
- And it says that it was granted to us before time began.

### Nazia please read:
